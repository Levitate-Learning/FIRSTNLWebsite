FIRST LEGO League Challenge

Ages 9 to 14

FIRST© LEGO© League introduces STEM (science, technology, engineering, and math) to children ages 9-14 (varies by region) through fun, exciting hands-on learning. Participants gain real-world problem-solving experiences through a guided, global robotics program using LEGO® technology, helping today’s students, families, and educators build a better future together.

In Newfoundland and Labrador, we host the annual provincial championships. Students from across the province come together to showcase their creations and ingenuity. The competition is open to schools and community groups in Newfoundland and Labrador.

The Challenge

1
The Robot Game

Build and program a LEGO Robot to complete tasks on the robot game table. The mission changes each year based on new and exciting themes!

2
Robot Design

Your team will do a 5 minute presentation showcasing the design of your robot and your teamwork to make it!

Costs and Funding

Our goal is to give every student in the province the opportunity to participate in STEM education. Cost is a major barrier for students across the province and we are committed to reducing costs and providing new and existing teams the resources they need to participate.

Every team in the province is required to pay a registration fee of $250 CAD. After paying this registration, we will provide you all the resources required for your team to participate in FIRST LEGO League.

Cost

$250

HST Included

Your Team Receives:

  • Participation at the FIRST Provincial Championship
  • SPIKE Prime Kit*
  • 2026 BIOGLOW Mat**
  • Travel Stipend***

Provided Travel Stipends for Teams

With your registration fee paid, you're team gets the opportunity to participate in the Newfoundland and Labrador Championship competition in St. John’s. We recognize that travel is another barrier for students to participate in programs like this. For teams that require overnight travel to participate, your registration fee will also unlock travel stipends to support getting to the competition.
