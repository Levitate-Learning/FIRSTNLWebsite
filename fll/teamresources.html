The following links are the instructions for youth registration. All participants are required to register through the FIRST dashboard. - Lead coaches only invite youth members that are 18 years old or older and can legally sign their consent form. - For youth members that are 17 years old and under, the lead coaches must send the Express Enrollment invitation/QR code directly to the parents/guardians to complete. There are 3 Links. - Youth registration instructions as a coach - Youth registration instructions for parents - Main FIRST page for youth registration.

A guided unit in the LEGO Spike app showing how to make a competition ready robot. There are multiple chapters in this unit and each chapter has a lesson plan for you to use: 1: Driving Around Controlling movements with the gyro sensor. 2: Playing With Objects Controlling movements using the distance sensor 3: Reacting to Lines Controlling movements using the color sensor 4: The Guided Mission An example solution for one of the robot game missions 5: Assembling an Advanced Driving Base Starter bot build guide. 6: My Code, Our Program Guide for writing easy to understand code 7: Time for an Upgrade Tutorial on making tools for your robot. 8: Mission Ready Final Chapter to help you get started preparing for the competition.

  • https://spike.legoeducation.com/prime/unit-plans/blt4b49bd6ee0bde119

Here is a wireframe guide to the FIRST Lego League mat provided by FIRST. You can use this to plan your programs for the 2.5 minute robot game. Can you determine how far your robot moves in one rotation of the driving wheels? With that knowledge you can use this wireframe guide to determine how many rotations you need to reach a mission!
