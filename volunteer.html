Help us inspire the next generation of innovators and leaders!

FIRST is more than just a competition, it's a community! FIRST competitions and events are not possible without the amazing help from our volunteers. You can help us inspire students through innovation, inclusion, team work, and fun! From a few hours to events all year long, every role helps students grow, learn, and thrive.

Confidently Inspire the Next Generation

We want all FIRST volunteers to feel confident in their roles. From judging to safety goggles and registration to field resets. We offer a variety of guidebooks and interactive training modules to help volunteers feel prepared for any role.

Creating a Safe, Supportive Experience

FIRST is committed to providing a safe, welcoming environment for every student. Through our Youth Protection Program, coaches, mentors, and volunteers are trained to support our robotics programs with high standards of care and respect.

A Role for Everyone

Whether you want to spend your time learning firsthand from our teams, helping plan and organize the event, or want to be right where the action is on the playing field, there is a role for you! Every role is critical to providing life changing experiences for youth in Newfoundland and Labrador. Learn more about the volunteer roles below.

Cross Program

Judge

As a judge, you'll play a key role in recognizing and celebrating team achievements by engaging directly with students through interviews and presentations. offering encouragement and constructive feedback along the way.

Meet the Teams

Cross Program

Referee

Referees observe matches and help foster fair gameplay. They identify and ‘call’ rule violations and assist competitors in understanding the rules of the game. This position requires additional training and intimate knowledge of game rules.

Meet the Teams Around the Field

Cross Program

Emcee

Bring the energy and spirit of FIRST to life as an Emcee, the enthusiastic voice that guides the audience through match play and team introductions. If you enjoy public speaking, connecting with teams, and creating a fun, inclusive atmosphere, this high-profile, fast-paced role is a perfect fit!

Robot Inspector

Robot Inspectors perform mandatory pre-match inspections and provide teams with guidance to ensure compliance with current season robot rules. They work under the supervision of the Lead Robot Inspector and use official checklists to determine pass/fail status.

Meet the Teams

Cross Program

Pit Admin

Pit Admin is a central point of contact for teams and guests in the Pit Area. In this role you will help teams understand the schedule, ensure teams know where to go, and direct teams to various competition officials to answer questions and get necessary support.

Event Delivery

Cross Program

Practice Area Supervisor

A Practice Field Supervisor plays an important role in helping teams prepare for competition by managing access to the practice field area, ensuring safety, and supporting fair and efficient field use.

Meet the Teams Around the Field

Cross Program

Quiet Room Attendant

The Quiet Room is a place for individuals to step away from the excitement and pressure of a FIRST event to decompress in silence. The Quiet Room Supervisor oversees the Quiet Room and helps to ensure that individuals using the room respect the nature of the space.

Event Delivery

Cross Program

Event Registration

Team Registration volunteers help check in teams at the beginning of the event and provide them with information and materials for the day. After the registration period has closed, the “registration desk” turns into the “information desk”.

Event Delivery

Cross Program

Scorekeeper

Scorekeepers operate the scoring software used during the event and are responsible for managing match data, rankings, and results displays. In this role accuracy, focus, and comfort with computers are essential.

Around the Field Event Delivery

Cross Program

Volunteer Room Supervisor

The Volunteer Room Assistant assists the Volunteer Coordinator with volunteer check in and monitors the volunteer room. This role is perfect for friendly, detail-oriented individuals who enjoy being part of a team and making others feel appreciated and informed.

Event Delivery

Cross Program

Audio/Video Crew

Be part of the action as Audio Visual Crew, where you'll help capture the excitement of a FIRST event through sound, lighting, video, and livestream support. This hands-on role is perfect for detail-oriented volunteers who enjoy tech, teamwork, and creating engaging experiences for both live and online audiences.

Event Delivery

Cross Program

Photographer

The Event Photographer captures the excitement and spirit of FIRST events by photographing teams, robots, volunteers, and key moments. This creative and collaborative role helps tell the story of the event through digital images.

Around the Field Event Delivery

Cross Program

Field Resetter

Field Resetters help keep the pace of match play by quickly and safely resetting the field after each match. They are essential to the flow of the event, working closely with the field team to ensure a consistent and safe competition environment.
